AutoClicker Visual interface

Light and calm in the style of macOS System Settings: grouped lists, a segmented control, real switches, a recording bar shaped like a capsule at the top of the screen, a circular progress ring and spring motion.

Interactive mock-up of the target interface. Open it in a browser and press Run and Record, flip the switches and hover the recording bar to see the intended motion. Exact values are in ui-spec.md.

Motion, everywhere the same

  • Springs: buttons shrink slightly when pressed and bounce back; the segmented control and switches slide with a gentle overshoot, like iOS.
  • During a run, each step shows a small spinner, then a check mark that draws itself; the ring in the run panel fills item by item.
  • Recording bar: a black capsule at the top of the screen. It widens on hover to reveal Undo and Finish, and the step counter pops on each click.
  • Messages ("Saved", "List is empty") rise from the bottom as a short snackbar, as on Android.
  • All of this can be built in PySide6: QPropertyAnimation with OutBack easing for the springs, and custom-painted switch, ring and spinner widgets.
